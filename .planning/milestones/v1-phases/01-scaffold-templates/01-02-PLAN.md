## Deviations from the spec

None introduced by this plan. It inherits the D-05/D-06 deviation documented in
`.planning/phases/01-scaffold-templates/01-01-PLAN.md` → "Deviations from the spec": the
templates live at `skills/dossier-init/templates/{en,fr}/`, not at a repository-root
`templates/`. Plan `01-06` amends spec §4 and `CLAUDE.md` accordingly.

Two spec-fidelity calls this plan makes explicitly, both recorded in `01-RESEARCH.md`'s
structural audit of the origin repository:

- **DISC-4** — spec §2.2 says `HISTORY.md` holds "commitments in progress (ours, theirs)"; the
  origin repository keeps a single flat commitments section. **The spec wins**: the template
  ships two labelled sub-headings. Divergence from the origin noted, low risk.
- **DISC-3** — the origin's `TONE.md` has two per-exchange sections, its answered-questions
  heading is dated in a non-ISO form, and `RELATIONSHIP.md` / `LEARNINGS.md` carry party-named
  headings. The templates ship **one** generic per-exchange section that grows by bullet,
  **undated** generic headings, and **role-generic** labels — counterpart names are unknown at
  scaffold time and are confidential besides (D-13).
