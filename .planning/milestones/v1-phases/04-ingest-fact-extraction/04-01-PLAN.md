## Script contract (fixed here; plan 04-04's SKILL.md and guard test rely on it)

- Filing form: `file_source.py --kind received|transcript|raw [--date YYYY-MM-DD] [--slug TEXT] PATH`.
  Statuses: `{"status":"filed","path":"sources/...","sha256":"...","bytes":N,"warnings":[...]}`,
  `{"status":"already_filed","path":"sources/...","sha256":"..."}`,
  `{"status":"error","error":"<code>","message":"...","hint":"..."}` (`hint` optional).
- Fetch form (first argument exactly `fetch`): `file_source.py fetch URL` ->
  `{"status":"fetched","path":"<absolute staged path>","ext":".pdf","bytes":N,"sha256":"...","final_url_host":"<host>","suggested_slug":"<slug>"}`.
- Error codes: `usage`, `no_dossier`, `bad_date`, `missing`, `not_a_regular_file`, `empty`,
  `url_argument`, `sources_outside_dossier`, `staging_inside_dossier`, `name_taken`, and for
  fetch `bad_url`, `unresolvable`, `not_public`, `redirect_refused`, `http_<code>`, `too_large`,
  `timeout`, `network`.
- Exit: 0 filed / already_filed / fetched; 1 refused or failed; 2 no `dossier.yaml` upward (same
  meaning as lint.py); 3 internal error (traceback-free message in the JSON).
- The D-02 hint sentence used by every download error: `Download it manually and re-run on the local file.`
