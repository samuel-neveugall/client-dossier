## Eval JSON reading (fixed here)

The `--json` document has `schemaVersion: 1`, `partial` and `partialReason`, `aggregates`,
`cases[].name`, `cases[].aggregates.score`, `cases[].arms.with[].error` and `costUsd`
(code.claude.com/docs/en/plugin-evals "JSON result"). A case score is the mean over runs of the
fraction of graders passed, so write-to-client at 1.0 means both of its graders passed in all three
runs. Extraction is one `python3 -I` call reading the file through argv; unknown fields are ignored;
a missing expected field is a gate failure, never a default.
