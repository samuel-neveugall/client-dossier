## Scenario contract (fixed here; plans 04-04 and 04-05 read it)

- Usage: `bash tests/scenarios/ingest-transcript.sh [--variant baseline|plugin|plugin-pointer]... [--runs N] [--prompt plain|pressured] [OUTDIR]`.
  Defaults: variants `baseline plugin`, 3 runs, prompt `plain`, OUTDIR `mktemp -d`.
- Named constants near the top, one per line in the form `NAME="value"` so tests can read them
  with sed: `INCOMING_REL="incoming/call-transcript.txt"`, `BACKDATE="2026-09-01"`,
  `SEEDED_LINE="- Jane Doe (Acme Consulting) set the phase one budget at 15,000 euros (mail 2026-10-01 from Jane Doe)."`,
  `OLD_AMOUNT="15,000"`, `NEW_AMOUNT="18,000"`, `NEW_PERSON="Tom Becker"`,
  `PROMPT_PLAIN="Here is the transcript of today's call: incoming/call-transcript.txt. What did we learn?"`,
  `PROMPT_PRESSURED="Here is the transcript of today's call: incoming/call-transcript.txt. What did we learn? Keep it short, I'm walking into my next meeting in two minutes."`.
- The transcript fixture, written with printf one line each: `Kickoff call, Acme Consulting`;
  `@00:04 Jane Doe: Thanks for joining. Tom Becker from our finance team is with me.`;
  `@00:19 Jane Doe: We confirmed the phase one budget at 18,000 euros.`;
  `@00:41 Tom Becker: Invoices go to accounts@acme.example, with the purchase order number on each one.`;
  `@01:05 Jane Doe: We need the first deliverable by 2026-11-14.`;
  `@01:32 Consultant: Understood, I will send the outline by 2026-10-24.`;
  `@02:10 Jane Doe: Maybe we extend to a phase two in January, nothing is decided.`
- Output: `OUTDIR/summary.tsv`, `OUTDIR/prompt.txt`, and per run `<variant>-<n>.jsonl`,
  `<variant>-<n>.diff`, `<variant>-<n>.lint`.
- summary.tsv has exactly these 20 tab-separated columns, in this order:
  1 variant, 2 run, 3 verdict, 4 prompt_kind, 5 agents_md, 6 filed_name, 7 timestamped_lines,
  8 refreshed, 9 lint_exit, 10 warnings, 11 ingest_skill, 12 extractor_dispatched, 13 corrected,
  14 filed_date_is_today, 15 model, 16 claude_code_version, 17 cost_usd, 18 is_error, 19 dossier,
  20 jsonl.
- Exit 0 every gate passes, 1 a gate fails, 2 harness error. Last line `SCENARIO ingest-transcript: PASS|FAIL`.
