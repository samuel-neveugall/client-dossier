## French scenario contract (fixed here; plan 05-05 runs it and reads its columns)

- Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 1 only. Not a pressure scenario (DRAFT-04's RED and GREEN belong to
  draft-reply.sh): plugin only, no baseline variant.
- Usage: `bash tests/scenarios/draft-deadline-fr.sh [--runs N] [OUTDIR]`. Defaults: 1 run,
  OUTDIR `mktemp -d`. Each run capped by SCENARIO_BUDGET_USD (default 1 USD), same SCENARIO_MODEL
  as draft-reply.sh.
- Named constants, one per line as `NAME="value"`; no value holds a straight double quote, `$`, a
  backtick, a backslash or a straight single quote (typographic apostrophes and guillemets used):
  - `ORDERING_PARTY="Acme Consulting|client|Jane Doe"`
  - `END_CLIENT="Northwind Bank|client final"`
  - `CONFIDENTIAL_LONG="Northwind Bank"`
  - `CONFIDENTIAL_SHORT="Northwind"`
  - `SLUG="reponse-date-audit"`
  - `DEADLINE="2026-10-30"`
  - `SIGN_OFF_HEAD="Bien cordialement,"`
  - `SIGN_OFF_NAME="Sam"`
  - `REFUSED_PHRASE="espère que vous allez bien"`
  - `TONE_RULE="- Répondre à la question dès la première phrase, rester sous 120 mots et finir par la formule « Bien cordialement, Sam » seule sur sa ligne (relecture de Sam le 2026-10-02)."`
  - `REFUSED_LINE="- « J’espère que vous allez bien » refusé le 2026-10-02 comme formule creuse (relecture de Sam le 2026-10-02)."`
  - `PENDING_LINE="- Jane Doe (Acme Consulting) a demandé le 2026-10-08 si l’audit de migration de données de Northwind Bank peut démarrer le 2026-11-02, et attend notre réponse (mail du 2026-10-08 de Jane Doe)."`
  - `NEVER_SAY_LINE="- Une date de démarrage avant que l’appel de cadrage ait eu lieu (décision du 2026-09-20 par Sam)."`
  - `FR_WORDS="le la les des du est pour nous vous que une et avec sur"`
  - `EN_WORDS="the and is we you to for with on of that"`
  - `PROMPT="dossier-draft mail reponse-date-audit : réponds à la question de Jane Doe sur la date de démarrage de l’audit ; le mail doit partir au plus tard le 2026-10-30."`
- One run: `dir=$(build_dossier --language fr --counterpart "$ORDERING_PARTY" --counterpart "$END_CLIENT" --confidential-name "$CONFIDENTIAL_LONG" --confidential-name "$CONFIDENTIAL_SHORT") || exit 2`;
  `seed_fixture "$dir"` (one call site, a line equal to `  seed_fixture "$dir"`) inserts each
  line as its own line after the blank and comment lines of the Nth `## ` heading, counted by
  position, never by its French title (05-CONTEXT discretion): TONE_RULE under the fifth of
  dossier/TONE.md, REFUSED_LINE under the sixth, PENDING_LINE under the first of
  dossier/HISTORY.md, NEVER_SAY_LINE under the second of knowledge/DOCTRINE.md; `check_fixture`
  dies (harness error naming the fixture) when a line is missing, when dossier.yaml does not read
  `language: fr`, or when its `confidential_names` line lacks a confidential name or names
  `Acme`; `strip_agents_pointer`; lint.py must exit 0 with `0 error(s), 0 warning(s)`, else
  harness error; `commit_baseline`; the run date (`date +%F`) before and after; `run_claude ... yes`;
  lint.py after the run into `fr-<n>.lint`.
- Output: `OUTDIR/summary.tsv`, `OUTDIR/prompt.txt`, and per run `fr-<n>.jsonl`, `fr-<n>.diff`
  (written as draft-reply.sh writes its `.diff`, untracked files' content included) and
  `fr-<n>.lint`.
- summary.tsv has exactly these 22 tab-separated columns, in this order: 1 variant (`plugin`),
  2 run, 3 verdict, 4 draft_file, 5 slug_kept, 6 header_ok, 7 status_value, 8 deadline_ok,
  9 french_body, 10 tone_applied, 11 leak, 12 history_line, 13 draft_skill,
  14 reviewer_dispatched, 15 reviewer_tools_ok, 16 lint_exit, 17 model, 18 claude_code_version,
  19 cost_usd, 20 is_error, 21 dossier, 22 jsonl.
  - draft_file, status_value, header_ok, leak, draft_skill, reviewer_dispatched,
    reviewer_tools_ok, lint_exit, model, claude_code_version, cost_usd, is_error: computed
    exactly as draft-reply.sh computes them, through the same lib-draft.sh and lib-scenario.sh
    helpers.
  - slug_kept: `yes` when the basename of draft_file is `<either run date>_` followed by SLUG and
    `.md`, else `no`.
  - deadline_ok: `yes` when `draft_header_field` of draft_file for `Deadline` equals DEADLINE.
  - french_body: the text of draft_file after its first line holding `-->`, lowercased and split
    into letter runs (Unicode letters, python3 `re.findall(r"[^\W\d_]+", ...)`); `yes` when the
    count of tokens in FR_WORDS is at least 5 and greater than the count in EN_WORDS. The helper
    is local to this script.
  - tone_applied: `draft_signoff_applied` of draft_file with SIGN_OFF_HEAD, SIGN_OFF_NAME,
    REFUSED_PHRASE.
  - history_line: `yes` when a line printed by `draft_history_bullets "$dir" "$base" <basename of draft_file>` also contains DEADLINE.
- Verdict: GREEN when draft_file is set and slug_kept, header_ok, deadline_ok, french_body,
  tone_applied, history_line, draft_skill and reviewer_dispatched are all `yes` and leak is `no`;
  INCOMPLETE otherwise. reviewer_tools_ok and lint_exit never gate.
- Harness errors (exit 2, `HARNESS ERROR:` on stderr, a `HARNESS_ERROR` row): `scenario_preflight`
  failure; missing result event; is_error true; fixture not seeded or not lint-clean;
  `client-dossier:dossier-draft` absent from the system/init skills or
  `client-dossier:doctrine-reviewer` absent from the system/init agents.
- Exit 0 when every run is GREEN, 1 otherwise, 2 on a harness error. Last line
  `SCENARIO draft-deadline-fr: PASS|FAIL`.
